## Augmented Reality (AR) Game
### My Role
Since this was my first time using Unity, the girls started with 3D models and their interactivity, whereas I focused on creating our UI, which I assumed would help me begin adjusting to Unity’s interface and getting to know my way around.

### Challenges and Learnings
The main part I found tricky was calling methods from scripts. I was able to trigger them in many ways through the interface as well as within the script itself, and sometimes I wasn’t sure which way was the best. Also, when the project got quite extensive and I discovered different ways to interact with the script, it became hard to trace back how different methods were called—e.g., where the audio was coming from and what triggered it. 😊

Another thing relevant to both projects is that we didn’t create any design of the domain for the scripts. We all had a good idea of what we were doing; however, along the way, we realized we needed more scripts to manage all functionalities. Sometimes, when working on two different features, we ended up with scripts handling similar functionalities but in different ways that later needed to be aligned.

## Virtual Reality (VR) Game
### My Role
In this project, I wanted to take on more responsibilities that required me to work with 3D models so I could also gain more experience with Blender. I was responsible for creating the packing station, which meant placing the cuts on the pizza according to the instructions on the customer’s ticket.

### Challenges and Learnings
The difficult part here was finding the best way for the user to split the pizza. Options included:
- Imitating cuts on the textures.
- Actually cutting the model during the game.
- Using a pre-split model and checking if the pizza cutter had collided with the opposite sides of the pizza.

Splitting the model during the game while tracking the player’s movements is quite tricky, and the same goes for live painting on the pizza texture. Therefore, the idea of pre-splitting the model and checking for collisions seemed like the best option.

Overall, while creating our VR game, I found myself caught up a lot in bugs coming from Rigidbody or the objects’ physics. Why are they floating? Why are they falling into the void? Why are they floating again? I asked myself these questions a lot during that project.
